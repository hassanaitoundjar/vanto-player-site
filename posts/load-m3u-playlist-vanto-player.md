# How to Load an M3U or JSON Playlist into Vanto Player

You've successfully downloaded Vanto Player, but when you open the app, the screen is empty. Don't worry—this is exactly how it is supposed to work! Vanto Player is a premium, blank-canvas media player. To start watching, you need to load your own M3U or JSON playlist.

This guide will show you how to securely upload your playlist via the web portal and sync it directly to your device, whether you are using Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, or a Smart TV.

## What You Need Before You Start

Before uploading, ensure you have the following ready:
1. **Your Vanto Player Device Details:** Open the Vanto Player app on your TV or mobile device. Note down the **MAC Address** and **Device Key** displayed on the screen.
2. **Your Playlist URL or File:** You need an active M3U link (URL), an Xtream Codes API login, or a local JSON/M3U file provided by your content provider.

## Step-by-Step Playlist Upload Guide

The easiest way to manage your playlists is by using a web browser on your computer or smartphone, rather than trying to type long URLs using a TV remote.

### 1. Access the Vanto Player Web Portal
Open a web browser on your computer or phone and navigate to the official Vanto Player website. Go directly to the playlist management or [Activation page](/activation).

### 2. Enter Your Device Credentials
In the portal, you will see fields asking for your device information. 
* Enter the **MAC Address** exactly as it appears on your TV screen.
* Enter the **Device Key** or pairing code.
* Click **Login** or **Connect**.

### 3. Add Your Playlist
Once logged into your device's profile on the web portal, look for the option to **Add Playlist**.

You generally have two options:
* **M3U URL (Recommended):** Paste the dynamic M3U link provided by your service. This is the best method because your channel list and VODs will update automatically whenever your provider makes changes.
* **Local File Upload:** If you have an `.m3u` file saved to your computer, you can upload it directly. Note that local files will not automatically update over time.

Give your playlist a recognizable name (e.g., "Sports Playlist" or "Main TV") so you can easily identify it inside the app.

### 4. Save and Sync
Click **Save** or **Add Playlist**. 

Now, look back at your TV or mobile device running Vanto Player. The app should detect the new playlist automatically. If it doesn't load immediately, look for a **Reload** or **Refresh** button on the Vanto Player home screen.

## Managing Multiple Playlists

One of Vanto Player's best features is its ability to handle multiple playlists simultaneously. If you have different sources for sports, movies, and international channels, simply repeat the steps above to add multiple M3U links. 

You can toggle between different playlists effortlessly from the Vanto Player main menu.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Why is my M3U playlist failing to load?**
If your playlist fails to load, double-check that you copied the URL correctly without any accidental spaces. Also, ensure that your provider's server is currently online. If the issue persists, try pasting the M3U link directly into a web browser to see if it downloads a file; if it doesn't, the link is broken.

**Can I upload an EPG (TV Guide) alongside my playlist?**
Yes. If your M3U link does not have an integrated guide, you can often add an XMLTV EPG URL in the same web portal where you added your playlist.

**Is it better to use an M3U URL or an Xtream Codes login?**
Both work well in Vanto Player. Xtream Codes logins (Username, Password, Server URL) are often preferred for faster loading times and better VOD categorization, while M3U URLs are more universally compatible.
