IPTV stands for **Internet Protocol Television**. Unlike traditional satellite or cable TV, which broadcasts content via radio waves or physical cables, IPTV delivers television programming and video content through the Internet. 

This means that instead of receiving TV programs as broadcast signals that enter your home from a rooftop antenna, satellite dish, or fiber-optic cable, you get them streamed through your internet connection.

## How Does IPTV Work?

With traditional TV, all programs are broadcast simultaneously. You can only watch the available programs when they are scheduled. If you want to watch something else, you change the channel and tune into a different frequency.

IPTV is different. Instead of broadcasting everything at once, IPTV services store content on servers and only send the specific program you request. 

Here's a breakdown of the process:
1. **Content Preparation**: Live TV signals or pre-recorded videos (VOD) are digitized and compressed (usually into MPEG4 format) so they can be streamed efficiently.
2. **Delivery**: The content is sent over your internet connection using IP (Internet Protocol). 
3. **Playback**: Your device (whether it's a Smart TV, a smartphone, or a computer running a media player like Vanto Player) receives the data packets, decodes them, and plays the video on your screen.

## Types of IPTV Services

There are three main types of IPTV services:

- **Live Television**: Broadcasting live TV shows or sports events as they happen, similar to traditional TV but over the internet.
- **Video on Demand (VOD)**: You select a movie or TV show from a catalog, and it starts playing immediately (like Netflix or Hulu).

- **Time-Shifted Media**: This allows you to watch broadcasts that you missed. "Catch-up TV" lets you view shows that aired hours or days ago.

## Why Choose IPTV?

- **Flexibility**: Watch what you want, when you want. You are no longer bound by traditional broadcasting schedules.
- **Multi-Device Support**: With players like Vanto Player, you can access your IPTV subscriptions on almost any device.
- **Modern IP Delivery:** Digital media streaming over IP networks offers efficient bandwidth utilization, allowing broadcast networks and authorized streaming platforms to deliver interactive features like time-shifting and multiple audio tracks.

1. A stable internet connection (preferably 10Mbps or higher for HD streaming).
