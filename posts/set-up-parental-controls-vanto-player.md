# How to Set Up Parental Controls in Vanto Player

When you load a comprehensive [M3U playlist](/blog/load-m3u-playlist-vanto-player) into a media player, it often includes thousands of channels and VODs from around the world. In many cases, these expansive playlists contain mature content, late-night programming, or specific categories that you may not want accessible to younger members of your household.

Fortunately, Vanto Player includes robust, easy-to-use Parental Control features. You can securely lock specific categories or completely hide unwanted content behind a PIN code. 

*Disclaimer: Vanto Player is a media management tool. It does not provide content. The effectiveness of parental controls depends on how your playlist provider categorizes their media.*

## Why Use Parental Controls?

A single playlist URL can populate your TV with everything from children's cartoons to mature movies. By setting up parental controls, you ensure that anyone using the TV can browse safely without accidentally stumbling upon inappropriate content.

Vanto Player allows you to:
* **Lock specific live TV groups** (e.g., locking a folder named "Adult").
* **Lock specific VOD categories** (e.g., Horror or Mature Movies).
* **Require a PIN** to access settings or delete playlists.

## Step 1: Creating Your Master PIN Code

Before you can lock any content, you need to establish your master security PIN.

1. Open the Vanto Player application on your device.
2. Navigate to the **Settings** menu (usually represented by a gear icon).
3. Scroll down and select **Parental Controls** or **Security**.
4. You will be prompted to create a new PIN. Enter a 4-digit numeric code that is easy for you to remember but difficult for children to guess.
5. Confirm the PIN by entering it a second time.
6. Make sure to toggle the main **Enable Parental Controls** switch to the ON position.

![Vanto Player Settings and Profile Screen](/screenshots/profile-screen.png)

*Tip: Do not use simple codes like 0000 or 1234, as these are easily bypassed.*

## Step 2: Locking Categories and Channels

Once your PIN is active, you can begin restricting access to specific parts of your playlist.

1. Return to the main screen of Vanto Player.
2. Navigate to your **Live TV**, **Movies**, or **Series** section.
3. Highlight the specific category group you want to restrict (e.g., a folder labeled "Mature").
4. Depending on your device (TV remote, touchscreen, or mouse), long-press the OK button, right-click, or use the 'Options' button on your remote to bring up the context menu for that category.
5. Select **Lock Category** or **Hide**.
6. The app will ask you to input your 4-digit PIN to confirm the action.

Once locked, a padlock icon will appear next to the category name. Whenever anyone tries to open that folder, Vanto Player will prompt them for the PIN. If they fail to provide it, the content remains invisible and inaccessible.

## Step 3: Managing and Resetting Your PIN

If you ever need to unlock a category permanently or change your security settings, simply return to the **Settings > Parental Controls** menu. You will need to enter your current PIN to access this area.

If you forget your PIN, you may need to clear the app data or uninstall and reinstall Vanto Player to reset the security settings.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Does locking a Live TV category also lock the VODs?**
No. Live TV, Movies, and Series are typically handled as separate databases. If you want to restrict adult content entirely, you must lock the relevant categories in all three sections.

**Can I hide a category entirely instead of just locking it?**
Yes, Vanto Player often provides the option to hide a group completely from the main interface. Check the category context menu for a "Hide" option to remove the folder from view.

**What happens if I update my playlist URL?**
If your provider renames a category (e.g., changing "Mature" to "Adult 18+"), the app may view it as a new category, and you will need to apply the parental lock to the newly named folder.
