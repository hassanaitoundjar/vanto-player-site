Not every television is a top-of-the-line Smart TV. If you have an older TV, a secondary display, or simply prefer controlling playback from your phone or laptop, [Google Chromecast](https://support.google.com/chromecast) is a practical solution.

Casting allows you to use your mobile device or browser as the controller while the TV functions as the display monitor. Here is how to cast media playlists to your Chromecast.

## The Prerequisites

To cast successfully, you need three things:
1. A Chromecast dongle plugged into your TV, or a TV with "Chromecast Built-in."
2. Your smartphone (Android or iOS) connected to the **exact same Wi-Fi network** as the Chromecast.
3. A casting-enabled media player app installed on your phone.

## Step-by-Step Guide

**1. Download a Casting-Enabled App**
First, download a media player that natively supports Google Cast. [**Vanto Player**](/download) supports casting protocols directly from Android devices and via Chromium-based desktop and web browsers.

**2. Load Your Playlist**
Open the app on your phone and input your M3U link or [Xtream Codes API](/blog/what-is-xtream-codes-api) login. Wait a few seconds for your Live TV, Movies, and Series to populate on your mobile screen.

**3. Connect to the TV**
At the top right corner of the Vanto Player mobile interface, tap the **Cast Icon** (it looks like a small TV screen with Wi-Fi waves). A menu will pop up listing all available devices on your network. Select your Living Room TV.

**4. Hit Play**
Navigate through your playlist on your phone. When you tap a channel or a movie, it will instantly beam to your television in full resolution.

## The Benefits of Casting

Casting is not just a workaround; it's a feature. 
* It saves the processing power of your TV, preventing menu lag.
* It allows you to search for movies using your phone's touch keyboard instead of a clunky TV remote.
* It lets you take your entire IPTV setup with you when traveling—just plug your Chromecast into a hotel TV and cast from your phone.
