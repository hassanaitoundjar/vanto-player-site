# Vanto Player Setup Guide: Xtream Codes API and M3U

Vanto Player is designed to be a premium, secure, and cross-platform media ecosystem. To keep your playlists secure and easy to manage, Vanto Player supports direct login right on your device.

If you have just downloaded Vanto Player and are stuck on the initial screen, you are in the right place. This guide will walk you through the setup process step-by-step.

*Disclaimer: Setting up Vanto Player unlocks the software features of the media player. It does not provide any media, live channels, or VOD content. You must use your own IPTV service credentials.*

## Step 1: Launch the Application

When you launch Vanto Player for the first time on any new device (such as an Amazon [Firestick](/blog/install-vanto-player-firestick), LG [Smart TV](/blog/install-vanto-player-smart-tv), or Android phone), you will be greeted by the login screen. 

![Vanto Player Login Options](/screenshots/welcome-screen.png)

This screen gives you multiple options to connect your service. The two most common and reliable methods are **[[Xtream Codes](/blog/what-is-xtream-codes-api) API](/blog/what-is-xtream-codes-api)** and **[M3U Playlist](/blog/load-m3u-playlist-vanto-player)**.

## Step 2: Choose Your Login Method

**Method A: Xtream Codes API (Recommended)**
Xtream Codes is the industry standard. It is generally faster, organizes Live TV and VODs perfectly, and handles the EPG (TV Guide) automatically.
1. Select **Xtream Codes API**.
2. Enter any name for your playlist (e.g., "My TV").
3. Enter your **Username** and **Password**.
4. Enter the **Server URL** provided by your service.

![Xtream Codes Login Screen](/screenshots/login-xtream-api-screen.png)

**Method B: [M3U URL](/blog/load-m3u-playlist-vanto-player)**
If your provider only gave you a long URL link ending in `.m3u` or `.m3u8`, you will use this option.
1. Select **M3U Playlist**.
2. Enter a name for the playlist.
3. Carefully type or paste the exact URL into the Playlist field.

## Step 3: Login and Sync

Once you have entered your details, click the **Add User** or **Login** button.

The app will connect to your provider and begin downloading the channel list, movie posters, and series information. This process can take a few minutes depending on the size of the playlist.

![Vanto Player User Profile Selection Screen](/screenshots/userlist-screen.png)

Once finished, you will see your profile. Select it to enter the Vanto Player dashboard!

## Troubleshooting Common Errors

**"Invalid Details or Login Failed"**
Double-check your typing. The most common mistake is confusing the letter 'O' with the number '0', or adding accidental spaces at the end of the Server URL. Ensure your subscription is active with your provider.

**The App is Stuck Loading**
If your playlist is extremely large (e.g., hundreds of thousands of VODs), it may take longer to load the first time. Please be patient. If it hangs completely, restart the app and try again.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Can I add multiple playlists?**
Yes, you can add multiple users or playlists and easily switch between them from the main menu.

**Is my data secure?**
Yes, your login credentials are saved locally on your device and are used securely to authenticate directly with your content provider's server. 

**How do I delete my playlists if I sell my device?**
Simply navigate to the settings or user profiles page within the app, select the playlist you want to remove, and click "Delete User".
