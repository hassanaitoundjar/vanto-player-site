# How to Install Vanto Player on Android TV and Smart TVs (Samsung, LG, Sony)

Watching high-quality content demands a high-quality player. Vanto Player is optimized for the big screen, providing a sleek, remote-friendly interface designed specifically for smart televisions. Whether you own an Android TV, a Samsung running Tizen, or an LG with webOS, setting up Vanto Player is a straightforward process.

*Disclaimer: Vanto Player is a standalone media player. It does not provide IPTV subscriptions, live channels, or on-demand content. You must add your own playlists to use the app.*

## Installing Vanto Player on Android TV (Sony, Hisense, TCL, Nvidia Shield)

If your television runs the Android TV or Google TV operating system, you have access to the Google Play Store, making installation incredibly simple.

1. Turn on your Android TV and navigate to the **Apps** tab or open the **Google Play Store**.
2. Navigate to the **Search** icon (magnifying glass).
3. Type **"Vanto Player"** using the on-screen keyboard or voice search.
4. Locate the official Vanto Player application in the search results and select it.
5. Click the **Install** button.
6. Once installed, click **Open** to launch the application and view your Device MAC address.

If you cannot find the app in the Play Store, you can always download the Android APK directly from our [Downloads page](/download) and sideload it using a USB drive or the Downloader app.

## Installing Vanto Player on Samsung Smart TVs (Tizen OS)

Samsung televisions run a proprietary operating system called Tizen. Vanto Player is natively supported in the Samsung ecosystem.

1. Press the **Home** button on your Samsung Smart TV remote.
2. Scroll to the left and select the **Apps** icon.
3. In the top right corner, select the **Search** icon.
4. Enter **"Vanto Player"**.
5. Select the app from the results and choose **Install**.
6. After the installation completes, you can add it to your Home screen for quick access.

## Installing Vanto Player on LG Smart TVs (webOS)

LG televisions utilize the webOS platform. Like Samsung, LG has its own dedicated application store where Vanto Player can be found.

1. Press the **Home** button on your LG Magic Remote.
2. Navigate to the **LG Content Store** (or "Apps" on newer webOS versions).
3. Select the **Search** icon at the top of the screen.
4. Type in **"Vanto Player"** and press search.
5. Select the Vanto Player app and click **Install**.
6. Launch the app to retrieve your device credentials.

## Next Steps: Linking Your Device

Once you have successfully downloaded and launched the application on your Smart TV, the screen will display a Device ID or MAC Address. You will need these details to sync your playlists from your computer or smartphone to your TV. 

Head over to the [Vanto Player Activation portal](/activation) to link your device and upload your content.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Does Vanto Player support 4K streaming on Smart TVs?**
Yes, Vanto Player is fully optimized to handle 4K UHD streaming. However, your viewing experience will depend heavily on the quality of your playlist provider and the speed of your internet connection.

**I can't find Vanto Player in my TV's app store. What should I do?**
App availability can vary by region. If you cannot find the app in your local Samsung or LG store, check if there is a region restriction. For Android TVs, you can bypass the store by sideloading the APK manually.

**Is there a difference between the Android TV and Samsung/LG versions?**
The core functionality, performance, and user interface remain consistent across all Smart TV platforms to ensure a unified Vanto Player experience.
